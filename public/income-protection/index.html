What Does Income Protection Cover?

It pays a regular monthly amount if you can’t work because of accident or illness, after the waiting period you choose.

Usually covered

  • Illness that stops you working
  • Injury from an accident
  • Longer-term sickness and recovery
  • Mental health conditions, where the policy covers them
  • Back and joint problems
  • Employed and self-employed workers

Not covered

  • Redundancy or unemployment
  • Resigning from your job
  • Retirement income
  • Anything excluded by the policy terms
  • Some pre-existing conditions
Important: Income Protection provides cover if you can’t work due to accident or illness. It does not cover redundancy or unemployment.

Questions & Answers

What happens after I complete the form?

An income protection expert will phone you to talk through your options and compare plans. Quotes are given over the phone.

Do I have to buy a policy?

No. The service is free and there is no obligation to take out a policy.

How much does Income Protection cost?

It depends on your age, occupation, health, how much income you want to protect and how long you can wait before payments start. The example above shows a starting price.

Can self-employed people get Income Protection?

Yes. Cover is available for self-employed people, contractors and business owners, subject to policy terms.

How much income can I protect?

Plans typically let you protect a proportion of your income, often up to around 70%, subject to insurer limits and policy conditions.

Does it cover redundancy?

No. Income Protection covers accident and illness only. It does not cover redundancy or unemployment.

Are pre-existing conditions covered?

This depends on the insurer and your medical history. Some conditions may be excluded or need extra checks.

Who can apply?

You need to be aged 18 to 59 and work 16 or more hours per week.
