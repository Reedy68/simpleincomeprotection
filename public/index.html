Frequently asked questions

What does income protection cover?

It pays a regular monthly income if illness or injury stops you working, after a waiting period you choose. Terms and exclusions vary by policy.

Does my employer’s sick pay mean I don’t need it?

Not necessarily. Employer sick pay varies, and it may reduce or stop after a set period, so check your contract. Income protection can pay a regular monthly income after a waiting period you choose.

Does income protection cover redundancy?

No. Income protection provides cover if you can’t work due to accident or illness. It does not cover redundancy or unemployment.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on your age, occupation, health, how much monthly benefit you want and how long you can wait before it starts paying.

What is a waiting period?

The time between you stopping work and the first payment. A longer wait usually means a lower monthly cost.

Who can apply?

People aged 18 to 59 who work 16 hours or more a week.
