What Does Income Protection Insurance Cover?

Income Protection Insurance is designed to provide a regular monthly income if illness or injury prevents you from working. It does not provide cover for redundancy, unemployment or voluntary resignation.

What Income Protection Usually Covers

Common reasons a valid claim may be accepted.

  • Illness preventing you from working
  • Accidental injury resulting in time away from work
  • Long-term sickness and recovery periods
  • Self-employed workers (subject to policy terms)
  • Monthly income replacement payments
  • Mental health conditions where covered
  • Back pain and musculoskeletal conditions
  • Many serious medical conditions

What Income Protection Does Not Normally Cover

Situations that are generally excluded.

  • Redundancy or job loss
  • Unemployment
  • Voluntary resignation
  • Business closure without illness
  • Retirement income
  • Claims excluded by policy terms
  • Certain pre-existing conditions
  • Situations outside policy conditions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Income Protection Insurance.

What is Income Protection Insurance?

Income Protection Insurance is designed to provide a regular monthly income if illness or injury prevents you from working. Cover is subject to insurer underwriting and policy terms.

Does Income Protection Cover Redundancy?

No. Income Protection Insurance covers illness and injury only. It does not provide cover for redundancy, unemployment or voluntary resignation.

How Much Income Can I Protect?

Most insurers allow you to protect a percentage of your income, subject to their underwriting limits and policy conditions. 70% is a typical amount of your monthly income that you can protect under this type of policy.

How Long Do Payments Last?

This depends on the policy selected. Some policies pay benefits for a limited period, while others may pay until retirement age if you remain unable to work.

When Does Cover Start?

Cover usually begins once the policy has been accepted by the insurer and the policy has gone into force.

Can Self-employed People Get Income Protection Insurance?

Yes. Many insurers offer Income Protection Insurance for self-employed workers, contractors and business owners.

Are Pre-existing Medical Conditions Covered?

This depends on the insurer and your medical history. Some conditions may be excluded or require additional underwriting.

Is Income Protection Insurance Worth It?

Many people rely on their income to pay mortgages, rent, utility bills and everyday expenses. Income Protection Insurance can provide financial support if illness or injury prevents them from working.

Which Insurers Do You Compare?

Available insurers may vary, but can include providers such as Aviva, LV=, Zurich, AIG, Legal & General and The Exeter.

Is There Any Obligation To Take Out A Policy?

No. Comparing quotes does not oblige you to purchase a policy.
